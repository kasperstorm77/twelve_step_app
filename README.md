## Features

### 📋 4th Step Inventory
Work through resentments, fears and harms in the familiar five-column form.

- Track resentments with cause, effects, and your part
- Use "I Am" perspectives to view situations from different roles
- Identify character defects and shortcomings
- Category filters for resentments, fears, harms, and sexual conduct

### 👥 8th Step Amends
Organize your amends list with a simple Kanban board.

- Three columns: To Do → In Progress → Done
- Add notes and details for each person
- Drag-and-drop to track progress

### 🌅 Morning Ritual
Start your day with a structured spiritual practice.

- Create custom ritual items (prayers, meditations, readings)
- Set timers with alarm/vibration when complete
- Track daily completion with timestamps
- View history to maintain consistency

### 🌙 Evening Ritual
End your day with reflection and review.

- Guided evening reflection questions
- Track what went well and what could improve
- Identify character defects that showed up
- Build awareness through daily practice

### 🙏 Gratitude Journal
Cultivate gratitude with daily entries.

- Quick and simple gratitude logging
- Review past entries by date
- Build a positive mindset over time

### 📖 Agnosticism Papers (Step 2)
Work through barriers to belief and develop your own conception of a Higher Power.

- Pair barriers with new powers/conceptions
- Track spiritual growth
- Build personal understanding

### 🔔 Notifications
Set custom reminders for your recovery practice.

- Daily or weekly schedules
- Customizable message content
- Enable/disable individual reminders

## Key Benefits

### 🔄 Google Drive Sync
Sign in with Google to automatically back up and sync your data across devices. Your recovery work is always safe and accessible.

### 🌍 Multi-Language Support
Available in **English** and **Danish**. Switch languages instantly from any screen.

### 🔒 Privacy First
- All data stored locally on your device
- Google Drive sync is optional
- No accounts required to use the app
- No ads, no tracking, no data collection

### ⚡ Seamless App Switching
All seven tools are integrated into one app. Switch between them instantly using the grid icon in the top bar.
